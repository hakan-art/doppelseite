Privacy

Privacy policy for the app “Doppelseite – Recipes” · Last updated: December 2026

In short: Doppelseite collects no data about you. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. Your recipes, weekly plan and shopping list are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We have no access to them.

What data the app processes

Doppelseite does not run any servers of its own. Everything you create in the app (recipes, your own photos, favorites, weekly plan, shopping list and settings) is stored locally on your device. We receive none of it.

Syncing through iCloud

If you are signed in with an Apple Account on your device and iCloud is allowed for Doppelseite, the app syncs your data through Apple’s CloudKit in the private database of your iCloud account. This data is stored by Apple and visible only to you; we as the provider can neither view nor analyze it. Processing by Apple is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. You can turn syncing off at any time in your device’s iCloud settings.

Importing recipes from the web

When you import a recipe by link (including via “Share” in Safari), the app fetches exactly that web page and its image directly from your device. As with any page visit, the website’s operator receives technical connection data such as your IP address. We have no influence on this processing; the website’s own privacy policy applies. Nothing passes through us.

Photos

To add your own recipe photo, the app uses the iOS photo picker. The app only sees the photo you choose and stores it with your recipe, on your device and, if applicable, in your iCloud account.

Notifications and timers

Timer notifications and the Lock Screen display (Live Activity) are scheduled on your device. We send no notifications and store no data for them.

No sharing, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party libraries that collect data. No data is shared with or sold to third parties.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since we store no data about you, we can usually only confirm that we hold nothing. You delete your data in the app by removing recipes or deleting the app; you manage your iCloud data in your device’s iCloud settings. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

This website

These pages are served through GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the pages securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are in GitHub’s privacy statement. We ourselves use no cookies, no analytics and no embedded third-party content on these pages.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that processes data differently, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
