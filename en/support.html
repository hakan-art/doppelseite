Frequently asked questions

How does the double page work on iPhone Duo?

When you unfold the Duo, a recipe shows the ingredients on the left and the steps on the right. Stand it half-open and cook mode opens, with the current step at the top and timers below. Folded, and on other iPhones, you switch between ingredients and steps with a toggle.

How do I import a recipe from the web?

In the recipe overview, tap “+” → “Import from link” and paste the link, or open the recipe in Safari and choose “Share” → “Doppelseite”. If a site doesn’t work, copy the recipe text and use “+” → “Paste from text”.

My recipes don’t appear on my other device.

Syncing runs through iCloud. On both devices, check that you are signed in with the same Apple Account and that iCloud is allowed for Doppelseite (Settings → your name → iCloud). The first sync can take a few minutes.

Why doesn’t a timer alert me?

Allow notifications for Doppelseite under Settings → Notifications → Doppelseite. Running timers also appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

My recipes are in German. How do I get the English ones?

The app follows your iPhone’s language. The English starter recipes are added when a new collection is created in English. Recipes you already have stay as they are, because they belong to you.

How much does Doppelseite cost?

Nothing. The app is free, with no ads and no account.

What data does the app collect?

None. See the privacy policy for details.
